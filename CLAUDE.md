# TSL: Social Conflict

Foundry VTT module — social conflict for D&D (dnd5e, a5e) inspired by **Thirsty Sword Lesbians**: you want something from an NPC who doesn't want to give it. **The conversation is played live** — the players talk in character, the GM answers as the NPC and plays their hidden nature; what the talk reveals and the emotions it leaves, the GM marks with no roll. **A roll only when the GM calls for it** (1–3 a conversation, often 1): say what the words really were — one of **eight maneuvers, each a pair of D&D skills**, under four buttons, the four social checks of D&D (I persuade: Appeal · Argument, all in: Seduce · Deal; I intimidate: Demand, all in: Blackmail; I lie: Deceive, told any way; I read them) — rolled in the system's own check window; the NPC's hidden **psychological nature** shapes every result: a key, a wall, a price, how they push back — and the **emotion** it leaves on whoever misses (five TSL emotions on everyone; a repeated one leaves a **scar**).

## Target platform
- **Foundry VTT v13** minimum, **v14 verified** (ApplicationV1 / Dialog V1 are deprecated-but-functional there; removal ~v16).
- Systems: `dnd5e` and `a5e` (Level Up). Vanilla JS + the Foundry API, no frameworks, no Handlebars — windows build raw HTML strings.
- Requires the `vtools` module (toolbar button).

## Design pillars (judge every change against these)
1. **Understand the person.** Knowing an NPC beats charisma. (Measured: a +2 character who knows the key beats a +7 face pressing blind.)
2. **What you say matters.** The line comes first; the maneuver is what the line really is (the GM holds them to it).
3. **The only mystery is the person.** Numbers are open (the DC is visible); the nature is hidden — but readable.
4. **Everyone at the table can contribute.** Everyone talks; one reads, another asks; skill pairs give the brute (Athletics) and the scholar (Investigation) maneuvers of their own; another character who really backed you in the talk helps (+2, they don't roll).
5. **Consequences outlive the scene.** Emotions last until acted out, scars for good; Strings and bonds carry into the campaign and into combat.
6. **The core fits on one screen.** Resist adding layers in response to "it feels thin" — first ask what decision the core loop is missing (that mistake built v2).
7. **The talk is played, not clicked.** The module keeps the person (hidden nature), remembers what the talk did, and counts the 1–3 rolls the GM calls for. Whatever the GM can decide from the roleplay takes no roll (the user, 2026-10-29).

## How it works
- **The talk is played; the roll is called** (v3.6). Players talk in character, the GM answers and plays the nature — the NPC's own Talk tab shows how they take each approach (key: `keyText`, wall: `wallText`, the third: a normal roll), the price, the no, the mask to act out. What the talk does, the GM marks there with NO roll: known facts (`data-known` chips) and emotions, on the NPC or on a PC on the map (`TSLEmotions.mark(actor, id, {toward})` — again = take it back quietly, so never a scar; Guilty needs `toward` and moves if marked toward another; one slim "The words landed" card). A roll only when the GM calls for it: usually one per conversation (the ask), a Read when the talk can't show it, at most 2–3.
- **Maneuvers** (`TECHNIQUES` in code; the UI says "maneuver" / "маневр") — no exchange object, no clocks, no turns. **No declared goal** (the user, 2026-10-13: "ціль в соціалці постійно міняється") — the goal is "get what you want from them, this time"; each ask is its own yes or no. Eight: an approach × a job (`techniqueFor(ap, job)`: three asks, three gambits), plus the lie and the Read.
  - **Skills** — every maneuver is a PAIR (`skills: [a, b]`): `bestSkill` picks the better for this actor (no picker), the other **backs it + the speaker's proficiency bonus** if `TSLTalk.proficient` (the user, 2026-10-31: it must grow with the character). Pairs: read ins+inv · demand itm+ath · appeal/seduce per+prf · argument per+his · deal (id `bargain`) per+ins · blackmail itm+inv · deceive dec+prf. The user (2026-10-29): "маневри мають бути прив'язані до навичок і їх комбінацій" — v1.21 had primary + support; v3.5's one-skill-each lost it.
  - **read** — `approach: null` (no key/wall die, no witnesses; of a bond only its edge/cost skill counts): the questions are **picked before the roll** in Talk (`_tQuestions`, two at most, in order — `opts.questions`; the user, 2026-11-09: «варіанти мають обиратися перед кидком»), the first answered on a hit, the second on a strong hit (`promptQuestions` only when none were passed); a miss costs no emotion. **A Read never moves Strings.** 8 questions: desire, fear, key, wall, price, pressed, mask (only if they show a face — `TSLTalk.openQuestions`), audience; plus two about the moment (`READ_MOMENT`, outside `READ_ORDER`: always open, nothing recorded): "What are they after right now?" (the Agenda, or the GM says) and "Are they telling the truth?" (the GM answers straight).

  - **ask** (demand/appeal/argument) — sized `ASK_SIZES` small −4 / costly 0 / against +4 ("against" needs a lever, a String or a bond ●●+ that matches);
  - **gambit** (blackmail/seduce/deal — the Deal's id is `bargain`), "All in" — any size, its own leverage (`needs`: fear/desire/price).
  - **the lie** (deceive; `job: "gambit"`, `approach: null`, `anyWay: true`) — not an approach: it takes the one it was told in (`approachOf(technique, told)`; form state `_tApproach`, passed as `approach` to preview/roll, in the payload to resolve). Key, wall, bond, emotions and scars all count for that way. No way given → a normal roll.
  - Each maneuver has a `line` (what it looks like in a scene), `win` / `miss` (plain words, shown before the roll), a `rule`. Maneuver buttons show the line and both skills (the rolling one bold, a tick on a backing one) — no odds; the odds sit only on the Roll button.
- **No openings** since v3.2 (a dead `flags.openings` may linger on old NPCs; nothing reads it); **no Prepare row** (a v3.6 draft, never released). Preparation is the talk itself (the GM's emotion mark).
- **Leanings — the archetype** (v1.8–v3.5, dropped in v3.6, back on 2026-11-07, as before on 2026-11-09): a character's dots in the three triads, kept in `socialFencing.triad` {power, attention, order} — `TRIAD_POINT_POOL` (4) to spread, `LEANING_MAX` (3) in one (`setLeaning`: the same dot again takes one off; past the pool, refused). A lead of 2+ is their archetype (`rulingTriad`: Power — Tyrant · Schemer · Duelist, etc.). Each dot: **+1 on the sheet** — one module effect (`flags.triadBonus`, `syncLeaningEffect`, `system.skills.<key>.bonuses.check`): Power → Intimidation, Emotion → Insight, Order → Deception, every check (the system's own window adds it; the preview's skill total already has it); and in the roll (`leaningMod`) +1 a dot on a maneuver of its way (Pressure · Heart · Reason; a lie: the way it's told), −1 on a way with no dots while leaning elsewhere (foreign ground). PCs only. On the first tab; counted in "Your maneuvers".
- **The talk target** is a token on the map **with the speaker**: Talk needs the speaker's token on the map in view (`_tokenOf`), drops a target that isn't there, and the NPC's "Ask them as" row needs the NPC on the map (the user, 2026-10-28: "лише ті, що є на карті з тобою"). The `targetToken` hook follows this user's Foundry targeting (T); "Pick on the map" uses the capture listener. No list.
- **Talk (a PC's) is the roll form** (v3.6 layout): right under the target's name, the Strings between you, always shown (`_holdChip`: ✓ String on them / ! String on you / a dash for none — the user, 2026-11-06: «має ОДРАЗУ показувати… якась галочка»); what the party knows in words + "Not known yet: … Watch how they take your words — or read them"; "Talk in character … Roll only when the GM calls for it"; four mode buttons, the four social checks of D&D, each showing its skill — **I persuade** / **I intimidate** / **I lie** / **I read them** (`_modeNow()`: ask | press | lie | read, from `_tTech` when one is picked, else `_tMode`). Nothing else to click until the GM calls a roll. I persuade (mode id `ask`) → "How did your words come at them?" (Heart or Reason, `data-t-ap`, each with how the person is KNOWN to take it: `TSLTalk.knownRelation` key → advantage, wall → disadvantage, plain → a normal roll, unknown) → "Plainly — or all in?" (the ask and the gambit of that approach) → the picked maneuver: "If it works / If not", "What are you asking for?", "Who's watching?", "Did your words play on what you know?" (levers), help, the Roll button with the odds (`TSLTalk.chance` / `hitChance`: party knowledge only, never the truth); the result says what to do next. I intimidate (the user, 2026-10-31: «А ЗАЛЯКУВАННЯ?») → always Pressure, so nothing to pick: the form opens at once on Demand, showing how they're known to take it; "Plainly — or all in?" (Demand or Blackmail); "What do you demand?". I lie (the user, 2026-10-30: «а де брехня?» — a lie isn't an ask) → "How do you tell the lie?" (all three approaches, in the lie's own words) → its form: "How big is the lie?", no levers. Sizes keep their DC shifts in each one's words (`TSLTalk.sizeOf`). Never call the third approach "plain ground" in the UI — it's a normal roll.
- **The stake: Strings** (resolve; 5+ since 2026-11-04, the same both ways): a sized roll (ask, gambit, lie) that's a **strong** win (5 over, or a 20) → a String on them (`_winHold` says why); a **botch** (5 under, or a 1) → a String on the speaker. A plain win or a plain miss moves none — but some wins cost one back (below). One each way, cap 1 (`STRING_CAP`) — at cap the card says "one at a time". A roll on which a String was spent (`stringSpent`, or the NPC's triad move) moves none. A Read moves none.
- **What a win costs** (applied in resolve) — **a roll never changes a bond** (neither its type nor its dots):
  - Pressure and Blackmail wins → no price, but they take a String on you ("They'll pay you back"); a Pressure win on a guilt type (Friend, Family, Lover, Protégé, Sworn, Confidant) with ● also leaves you Guilty toward them;
  - a Deal → your word: a String on you until it's kept; a price that is a debt → a String on you;
  - Appeal/Argument/Deal plain hits → the nature's `price` prompt (`hasPrice`; a Deal's says "Give your word"). Heart misses → their emotion (as every miss).
- **Bonds** (restored from v1.52–v2.0 on 2026-11-04; v2's guard and aura strengths on 2026-11-09 — the user: «там було НАБАГАТО БІЛЬШЕ»): every bond's powers are in sight in its row (per ●, their guard, the aura, ●●, ●●●; only caption and notes fold). THEIR bond toward the speaker is their **guard** (`guard`: −1 for those close — DC −● when you ask them, +1 for an Enemy — DC +●; on a Read too). A TYPE (who you are to each other — the story's; the GM sets it, or the player who invests a String in a fresh bond) and a STRENGTH 0–3 ● (how deep it runs). Per ●, against that person (`bondParts`): +1 on the type's approach (`school`); +1 when its `edge` skill rolls (not on top of the approach); −1 when its `cost` skill rolls; a hostile type (Rival, Enemy) −1 on any other approach. A Read has no approach — only edge and cost count there. Within `bondAuraRange` ft of each other, in a fight or not: the type's aura (`bond-powers.js`; each lever ±1, ±2 at ●●●, summed over bonds, capped ±2). ●●: the type's ability (GM text, mostly once per scene). ●●●: its signature, once per long rest (`sigUsed` on this side; cleared by `dnd5e.restCompleted` longRest / `a5e.actorRest` long, or the GM's "Ready it"); with `fx` it goes on the sheet until the bearer's next roll, without it the GM plays it. Dots grow only by invested Strings (the player) or the GM's hand; `shift` (warm/cool) moves the dots of a typed bond; `begin` gives a type where there's none or a Stranger.
- **Gambits:**
  - Blackmail needs known Fear (+2 lever) or a held String → hit: they're Frightened, and they take a String on you (whether they're your Enemy now is the GM's call); miss: their emotion.
  - Seduce needs known Desire (+2) or a bond ●● → hit: they do it for you; miss: Hopeless.
  - The Deal (its known Price +2 lever, needs nothing) → you give your word: on record (`flags.deals`) + one String on the speaker (even if the price is a debt); a strong hit is on your terms, a plain one on theirs (give your word or walk). The GM's `settleDeal` on the NPC's Talk tab: Kept → their String goes, strangers become Allies ● (`begin`); Broken → the NPC keeps theirs, the speaker's on them burns, strangers become Enemies ●● — any other bond is the GM's to change. A miss: "No deal", only the emotion (a firm nature may close the door).
  - Deceive (told any way, needs nothing) → hit records a lie (`flags.lies`; the liar sees "They believe your lie" in Talk); the GM's `exposeLie` → the liar's String on them burns, they take one on the liar, strangers become Enemies ●●; miss → their emotion + door closed (any nature).
- **Witnesses** (asks & gambits): the player picks In private / Before witnesses (`_tWitnesses` → `witnesses`). The nature's `audience` (+2 bend / −2 harden / 0, `audienceWhy`) is added AFTER the dice like key/wall (the preview never consults it); `known.audience` learned. No String of their own (since 2026-11-02 the win gives one anyway); a public no → Insecure ("loses face", unless the nature already hurt with Insecure). The GM can make a whole scene public (scene flag `public`, `TSLTalk.publicScene()`, the button in the Social Scene header): `_tWitnesses` null = follow the scene; the speaker's click overrides.
- **Masks**: `socialFencing.maskId` = the nature they SHOW (GM: Profile → Nature → Mask). `getShown` (any face) / `getMask` (a face ≠ their nature). Players see "seems: X" + "How they seem" (Talk, Scene); key/wall/price are always the true nature's. The mask question names an open face at once; a mask is known as a mask; key+wall known → named → "The mask falls". Default (no maskId) = "Nature unread", as before.
- **Strings** = what you have on someone: a debt, a weakness seen, a line crossed before others. **One at a time each way** — `countOn` never shows more than one, `spend` takes every copy, and old worlds' lists (up to three on one person) are cut on load (`trimToCap`). The first tab lists the Strings at hand (`_stringsSection`: who on this map, with Remind / Strike +5 / Guard +5 AC / Invest; who here holds one on you; who else, off the map). **Guard** is +5 AC for a round — until the start of the holder's next turn (or the end of the fight); Strike ends after the holder's next roll. Yours on them: a strong win of a sized roll, the GM's + (they open up to you). Theirs on you: a bad miss; and a win that costs one — Pressure/Blackmail, a Deal (until kept), a price that is a debt. Exposed lie: the liar's String burns, they take one. A broken word: theirs stays, the speaker's burns. Spending yours: **remind** them (they agree, no roll; against their interests — the GM names the price), +5 after a miss, **invest** (+1 ●, hostile −1 ●; fresh bond — the player picks the type), Pull, **Quits** (answer their move: both burn). A player raises dots only by investing (lowering is free); the GM sets them by hand; the "+" is the GM's. `TSLStringStore.add` makes a **Stranger** bond when none exists; investing a String in it (or the GM) gives it a type.
- **NPC moves** (`abilities.js`): each costs the NPC's String on that character. The triad's at the roll — `TSLAbilities.promptMove` on the GM in resolve, after the dice (Power any sized roll: −5 and re-grade; Emotion a win that stands: their `hurts` on the speaker; Order a win with no price paid already: their price). The nature's two from the NPC's Talk tab (`_movesSection`, "Pull it on: [PC]") → a chat card waiting for the answer (`by: "pc"` the PC's owner or GM; `"gm"` the GM only; Quits if the PC holds one on them); fx: inspire (`system.attributes.inspiration`), keep, flip, silence (+4 DC in preview until lifted), emotion, shift, pick friend/npc/lie (GM dialog), transfer. A move is a tell; under a mask they move as their true nature.
- **Roll** (speaker's client, `TSLTalk.roll`): `preview()` builds the visible math (the better skill + proficiency if trained in the other + the Leaning of that approach + bond ● + lever + help ± emotions vs DC `10 + prof + max(WIS, INT)` + dial + size). It never consults the nature. The system's check window opens by default (`systemRollDialog` true), set to the skill with the module's bonus as a situational mod. A KEY approach rolls a second d20 and keeps the better, a WALL keeps the worse — on the ask, the gambit and a lie told that way (a Read has no approach). Into a WALL the support doesn't count either (`wallLost`, after the dice; the user's pick, 2026-10-31: support doubles proficiency, so without this the best-trained face rolled 72% into a wall). `tools/harness.js` holds balance targets for levels 1–17; a rule change must stay inside them. Grades: strong (DC+5 / nat 20), success, failure, botch (DC−5 / nat 1). A miss may burn a String (+5). Then `TSLGMActions.request("talkResolve", payload)` with `technique`.
- **Resolve** (GM only): re-derives key/wall from the truth, records knowledge.
  - The stake (above): a strong win → a String on them; a botch → one on the speaker; one each way per roll; none when a String was spent on it. A firm nature refusing a big ask/gambit closes the door.
  - Names the nature once key AND wall are known. ONE card, then a pulse `{technique, approach, job, grade, paid, rel, ended, granted, witnesses}`.
- "The door is closed for the scene" / "they won't be read again this scene" are narrative — the GM holds them.
- **Emotions & scars** (`emotions.js`): an emotion is an AE with `statuses: ["tsl-emo-<id>"]` + `flags.emotion {id, by, toward}` (a HUD toggle without the flag counts too). `rollParts(src, tgt, technique)` adds −2 for the speaker's own (Angry→Heart, Frightened→Pressure, Insecure→Reason, Guilty→everything vs `toward`, Hopeless→ask/gambit), ±1 for scars, +2 for the target's (`opens` approach; Guilty only for its `toward`). Sources: the GM's mark from the talk (`mark`, no roll); every miss except a Read → the nature's `hurts` (Seduce → Hopeless); Blackmail → NPC Frightened; exposeLie → NPC Angry; an NPC's move, refused. A PC taking one they carry → the scar (once). Pressure wins also give the NPC a String on the speaker. Clearing: × on the tag (owner/GM) → act it out or a friend's comfort → one chat line. **Shown at once:** the strip at the top of every Chronicle tab — emotions, scars and the system's conditions; the palette (Change — the GM on anyone, a player on their own character) puts any emotion on or off (no roll; a slim "The GM marks it" / "Marked by its player" card on, nothing off), any scar on a character (`addScar`, a card) and any condition (`TSLConditions.toggle`) — × on a scar asks first. All in that's locked (Blackmail without a known Fear or a String; Seduce without a known Desire or ●●) says what's missing and how to get it.
- **Knowledge:** `socialFencing.known {desire,fear,key,wall,price,pressed}` + `revealed` — shared by the party, kept for good. Players never see the archetype (or the GM's dossier) until it is named; the GM sees the truth marked "hidden".

## Data on actor flags (scope `tsl-social-conflict`)
- `socialFencing` — `{ archetypeId, maskId (the nature they show), revealed, known (+ mask, audience), stance (after a no: nature|yield|firm|ask), intent (Agenda — GM text, what they want from the party), notes, title (a caption under their name on the map), silence ([pcIds] the Hermit shut out), points {desire, fear, mask (labelled "Face" since v3.3), line} }`. `triad` — the Leanings {power, attention, order}, read again since 2026-11-07. Archetype ids are legacy-stable (`machiavellian` = Schemer, `exalted` = Idol, `dogmatic` = Zealot).
- `bonds` — `[{ id, targetActorId, type, attitude (= strength 0..3), label (the map caption, shared), notes (private), sigUsed (this side's ●●● spent until a long rest) }]`, mirrored (type, strength, label).
- `stringList` — `[{ id, label, targetActorId }]` — at most one per target.
- Scene flag `board` — `{ pos: [{id, x, y}], notes: [{id, x, y, text}] }` (the GM's arrangement and the captions); the Web of Bonds keeps the same in the hidden world setting `worldBoard`. A player's own arrangement: localStorage.
- Chat message flag `ability` — `{ npcId, pcId, abilityId, open }` — an NPC's move waiting for its answer.
- `openings` — dead since v3.2 (v3.0–3.1 moods); nothing reads it.
- `lies` — on NPCs: `[{ id, byId, at }]`, until the GM brings one out.
- `deals` — on NPCs: `[{ id, byId, price (on their terms), at }]`, until the GM marks it Kept or Broken.
- ActiveEffects: the Leanings on the sheet (`flags.triadBonus`, one per character, kept in step with the dots); emotions (`flags.emotion`, status `tsl-emo-*`) and scars (`flags.scar`) — `migrateToV3` leaves them alone; the bond aura (`flags.bondAura`, status `tsl-bond-aura`, rebuilt by the active GM) and a signature's moment (`flags.bondMoment`, status `tsl-bond-moment`, gone after the next roll).
- `exchange` / `encounter` / `willpower` / `woundTrack` — dead; `migrateToV3` unsets them.

## Hard-won platform lessons (still true)
- **`"socket": true` in module.json** is what makes Foundry relay `module.<id>` socket events. Without it every player → GM message is silently dropped. Changing it, or adding a new script file to the manifest, needs a **world restart**, not F5.
- **Refresh, don't re-render on everything.** The Chronicle listens to updateActor (only `flags.tsl-social-conflict`, name, img; `system` only on Talk), Active Effect hooks for its actor and target, updateScene, deleteActor — all through `_refresh()`: coalesced (60 ms) and held while a text field in the window has focus (a re-render wiped half-typed text). The Scene re-renders only for our effects. Keep new hooks on this path.
- **A bond aura is never left behind.** Anyone off the map loses it (the cleanup pass in `_refresh`). It works everywhere, in a fight or not (the user's call, 2026-11-06) — so its check and save bonuses count in social checks through the system window too.
- **The relay trusts no one's character but their own:** `TSLGMActions.handle(data, senderId)` checks the sender owns the actor `_actorOf` names (`srcId` / `holderId` / `actorId` / `pcId`; `stringSet` also checks the pcId is the holder or the target). A new GM action needs its actor in `_actorOf` — or a place in `OPEN` when it speaks for no one's character (`boardPatch`: captions on the shared board). With no GM online, `request` warns and returns false.
- **A player can't write an NPC's flags** — anything mirrored onto one (a bond's other side) goes through the GM, or it silently never happens (that was the bond desync before 2026-11-02).
- **Who is a PC:** always `SocialArchetypeManager.isPC(actor)` = type `"character"` OR player-owned — never `hasPlayerOwner` alone. The author tests alone as the only (GM) user, where nobody owns the characters; with `hasPlayerOwner` every character read as an NPC and Talk showed the GM cheat sheet instead of the ask (2026-10-14).
- The Chronicle normalises to the **world actor** (`game.actors.get(actor.id)`) — unlinked tokens hand over synthetic actors with token-local flags.
- Canvas picking uses a **DOM capture listener on `#board`** + `canvas.canvasCoordinatesFromClient` (PIXI 8 removed `canvas.app.view`).
- **a5e**: `system.bonuses.*` are RecordFields — a flat AE does nothing; numeric bonuses go through `flags.a5e.effects.bonuses.<kind>` (CUSTOM mode, the bonus record as JSON; list all four `attackTypes`). a5e roll-mode keys are `rollMode.{attack,abilityCheck,abilitySave,skillCheck}.all` (not `savingThrow`). AC: `system.attributes.ac.changes.bonuses.value`. dnd5e: `system.bonuses.*` / `system.attributes.ac.bonus`.
- Foundry gives `<button>` a stretchy base — every button needs an explicit width strategy.
- Look before claiming a UI works: `tools/preview.js --shoot` (portraits via env `TSL_PREVIEW_PORTRAITS`, never commit local paths). After an update mid-session, F5 — stale clients look broken.

## UI conventions
- CSS variables `--tsl-*` (dark: `--tsl-bg #150c0f`, text `#f0dfc8`, accent `#e8557a`); approach colours Pressure `#e8557a`, Heart `#9b6ee8`, Reason `#55b8e8`; gold `#e0b45e` (`--tsl-sway`, a legacy name) for sizes, prices and strong results; red `#d0574d` for walls and bad results. Fonts: Cinzel titles, EB Garamond body (with fallbacks).
- **No emoji in UI** — Font Awesome icons (key = `fa-key`, wall = `fa-door-closed`, read = `fa-eye`).
- Dialogs use `tslDialogOptions()` (dark theme). One card per roll; the result also shows where Roll was clicked.
- **Every word on screen goes through `_t("English", {placeholders})`** — a plain double-quoted literal (the harness scans for them). A new string needs its Ukrainian in `scripts/i18n-uk.js` (the harness fails on a missing key or a lost {placeholder}). Data texts (natures, techniques, emotions, bonds…) are translated in place by `TSLi18n.apply()` — add new data fields to it. Ukrainian style: the NPC is "вони" (no gendered verbs), the player "ти"; terms Струна, Зв'язок, Натура, Маска, Ключ/Стіна, При свідках, Просити/Ва-банк/Читати, СК, КБ. Chat cards are written in the language of the client that creates them.
- Fonts: Cinzel / EB Garamond aren't loaded — the stacks fall back to Modesto Condensed / Signika (Latin only), then Palatino Linotype / Georgia for Cyrillic. Keep Cyrillic-capable fallbacks; Ukrainian labels are longer — check `--lang=uk` screenshots.
