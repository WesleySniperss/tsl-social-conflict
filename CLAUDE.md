# TSL: Social Conflict

Foundry VTT module — social conflict for D&D (dnd5e, a5e) inspired by **Thirsty Sword Lesbians**: you want something from an NPC who doesn't want to give it. **The conversation is played live** — the players talk in character, the GM answers as the NPC and plays their hidden nature; what the talk reveals and the emotions it leaves, the GM marks with no roll. **A roll only when the GM calls for it** (1–3 a conversation, often 1): say what the words really were — one of **ten maneuvers, each a pair of D&D skills**, under four buttons, the four social checks of D&D (I persuade: Appeal · Argument, all in: Seduce; I intimidate: Demand, all in: Blackmail; I lie: Deceive, told any way; I read them: Watch · Provoke · Confide · Question) — rolled in the system's own check window; the NPC's hidden **psychological nature** shapes every result: a key, a wall, a price, how they push back — and the **emotion** it leaves on whoever misses (five TSL emotions on everyone; a repeated one leaves a **scar**).

- Systems: `dnd5e` and `a5e` (Level Up). Vanilla JS + the Foundry API, no frameworks, no Handlebars — windows build raw HTML strings.

## Design pillars (judge every change against these)
1. **Understand the person.** Knowing an NPC beats charisma. (Measured: a +2 character who knows the key beats a +7 face pressing blind.)
2. **What you say matters.** The line comes first; the maneuver is what the line really is (the GM holds them to it).
3. **The only mystery is the person.** Numbers are open (the DC is visible); the nature is hidden — but readable.
4. **Everyone at the table can contribute.** Everyone talks; one reads, another asks; skill pairs give the brute (Athletics) and the scholar (Investigation) maneuvers of their own; another character who really backed you in the talk helps (+2, they don't roll).
5. **Consequences outlive the scene.** Emotions last until their drive is done (Inspiration when it is) or a long rest; Strings and bonds carry into the campaign and into combat.
6. **The core fits on one screen.** Resist adding layers in response to "it feels thin" — first ask what decision the core loop is missing (that mistake built v2).
7. **The talk is played, not clicked.** The module keeps the person (hidden nature), remembers what the talk did, and counts the 1–3 rolls the GM calls for. Whatever the GM can decide from the roleplay takes no roll (the user, 2026-10-29).

## How it works
- **The talk is played; the roll is called** (v3.6). Players talk in character, the GM answers and plays the nature — the NPC's own Talk tab shows how they take each approach (key: `keyText`, wall: `wallText`, the third: a normal roll), the price, how they hit back, what they want and fear (theirs, or their nature's when the dossier is empty). What the talk does, the GM marks there with NO roll: known facts (`data-known` chips) and emotions, on the NPC or on a PC on the map (`TSLEmotions.mark(actor, id, {by})` — again = take it back quietly, so never a scar; one slim "The words landed" card). A roll only when the GM calls for it: usually one per conversation (the ask), a Read when the talk can't show it, at most 2–3.
- **Maneuvers** (`TECHNIQUES` in code; the UI says "maneuver" / "маневр") — no exchange object, no clocks, no turns. **No declared goal** (the user, 2026-10-13: "ціль в соціалці постійно міняється") — the goal is "get what you want from them, this time"; each ask is its own yes or no. Ten: an approach × a job (`techniqueFor(ap, job)`: three asks, two gambits — Pressure's and Heart's; Reason has none since the Deal went on 2026-11-15 — three reads), plus the lie and Watch (the read with no approach).
  - **Skills** — every maneuver is a PAIR (`skills: [a, b]`): `bestSkill` picks the better for this actor (no picker), the other **backs it + the speaker's proficiency bonus** if `TSLTalk.proficient` (the user, 2026-10-31: it must grow with the character). Pairs: watch (id `read`) ins+inv · provoke itm+ins · confide per+ins · question inv+his · demand itm+ath · appeal/seduce per+prf · argument per+his · blackmail itm+inv · deceive dec+prf. The user (2026-10-29): "маневри мають бути прив'язані до навичок і їх комбінацій" — v1.21 had primary + support; v3.5's one-skill-each lost it.
  - **four ways to read** (2026-11-14, `READ_WAYS`; the user: «Мало прийомів на інсайт, вони одноманітні і не цікаві»): **Watch** (id `read`, ins+inv, no approach, a miss costs nothing — truth, then intent), **Provoke** (itm+ins, Pressure — wall, then fear; miss: their emotion on you), **Confide** (per+ins, Heart — key, then desire; miss: a String on you), **Question** (inv+his, Reason — audience, then price; miss: on their guard — `wary`, +`WARY_DC` (2) on that speaker's next roll against them, then it drops). HOW you read IS what you look for (2026-11-20, the user's pick: «Спосіб і є питання»; `asks` = two, in order; `openQuestions(npc, technique)` skips what's known; `readFinds`), who is good at it, and what a miss costs (`missCost`). A read by an approach is said that way: the key/wall die (and the dice show it), the Leaning, the bond, the emotions of that way. None wins a String. The form: I read them → "How do you read them?" (a 2×2, Watch picked at once). Don't fold them back into one Read: one list of questions was "monotonous".
  - **read** (what all four share) — Watch has `approach: null` (no key/wall die, no witnesses; of a bond only its edge/cost skill counts). **Nothing to pick** (3.21; the 2026-11-09 pick-before-the-roll list was «важко і не зрозуміло»): a hit finds the way's first thing still unknown, a strong hit both (`readFinds`); what a miss costs is the way's own (`missCost`: nothing · their emotion · a String on you · on their guard). **Told straight** — all of it, on the card and on record (3.16–3.20's "shown, the players name it" whisper for desire, fear and price is gone). **What the players see** (2026-11-14): every answer but "is it true?" carries a standard line of `READ_HINTS` for the TRUE nature and that question (`pickHintKey`, one not yet seen) — for what they want, fear or are after with nothing of the GM's own written (`ownFact`: Desire, Fear, Agenda), it IS the answer ("By the look of it:"); the GM's own words are told straight instead; the lines are kept with the signs. **No read wins a String** (only Confide's miss gives one — to them). **A read that hits also notices a sign** (`pickTellKey` → `ARCHETYPE_TELLS`, 9 × 40, veiled; `sign` on the card: “You notice: …”) — always the TRUE nature's; evidence, kept in `socialFencing.signs` (the deduction loop, below). The pool is content the user asked for (2026-07-19) — keep it shown. 6 questions: desire, fear, key, wall, price, audience; plus two about the moment (`READ_MOMENT`, outside `READ_ORDER`: always open, nothing recorded): "What are they after right now?" (the Agenda, or a standard line) and "Are they telling the truth?" (the GM answers straight).

  - **ask** (demand/appeal/argument) — sized `ASK_SIZES` small −4 / costly 0 / against +4 ("against" is never locked — `preview().bareAsk` makes the form say when nothing on record backs it; the user, 2026-11-14: «Так, зніми замки»);
  - **gambit** (blackmail/seduce), "All in" — any size, its own leverage (`needs`: fear/desire). **No Deal** (removed 2026-11-15 — the user: «Сам концепт баргаіну для мене дуже складний тут і не має особливого сенсу» → «Прибрати угоду»): Reason opens its Argument at once, with no "Plainly — or all in?"; a promise is part of the talk, and what it's worth is the GM's call. Old worlds' `flags.deals` are dropped on the GM's load. Don't bring back a "your word" mechanic without a reason from play.
  - **the lie** (deceive; `job: "gambit"`, `approach: null`, `anyWay: true`) — not an approach: it takes the one it was told in (`approachOf(technique, told)`; form state `_tApproach`, passed as `approach` to preview/roll, in the payload to resolve). Key, wall, bond, emotions and scars all count for that way. No way given → a normal roll.
  - Each maneuver has a `line` (what it looks like in a scene), `win` / `miss` (plain words, shown before the roll), a `rule`. Maneuver buttons show the line and both skills (the rolling one bold, a tick on a backing one) — no odds; the odds sit only on the Roll button.
- **No openings** since v3.2 (a dead `flags.openings` may linger on old NPCs; nothing reads it); **no Prepare row** (a v3.6 draft, never released). Preparation is the talk itself (the GM's emotion mark).
- **Leanings — the archetype** (v1.8–v3.5, dropped in v3.6, back on 2026-11-07, as before on 2026-11-09): a character's dots in the three triads, kept in `socialFencing.triad` {power, attention, order} — `TRIAD_POINT_POOL` (4) to spread, `LEANING_MAX` (3) in one (`setLeaning`: the same dot again takes one off; past the pool, refused). A lead of 2+ is their archetype (`rulingTriad`: Power — Tyrant · Schemer · Duelist, etc.). Each dot: **+1 on the sheet** to two of the four social checks — one module effect (`flags.triadBonus`, `syncLeaningEffect`, `leaningSheet`, `system.skills.<key>.bonuses.check`): Power → Intimidation + Deception, Emotion → Insight + Persuasion, Order → Deception + Persuasion (`TRIAD_SKILLS`; a skill two triads share sums their dots, capped at 3; the user, 2026-11-15: «крапки архетипів посилюють все крім звичайного переконання» — no skill beyond the four buttons' own), every check (the system's own window adds it; the preview's skill total already has it); and in the roll (`leaningMod`) +1 a dot on a maneuver of its way (Pressure · Heart · Reason; a lie: the way it's told), −1 on a way with no dots while leaning elsewhere (foreign ground). PCs only. On the first tab; counted in "Your maneuvers".
- **The talk target** is a token on the map **with the speaker**: Talk needs the speaker's token on the map in view (`_tokenOf`), drops a target that isn't there, and the NPC's "Ask them as" row needs the NPC on the map (the user, 2026-10-28: "лише ті, що є на карті з тобою"). The `targetToken` hook follows this user's Foundry targeting (T); "Pick on the map" uses the capture listener. No list.
- **Talk (a PC's) is the roll form** (v3.6 layout): right under the target's name, the Strings between you, always shown (`_holdChip`: ✓ String on them / ! String on you / a dash for none — the user, 2026-11-06: «має ОДРАЗУ показувати… якась галочка»); what the party knows in words + "Not known yet: … Watch how they take your words — or read them"; "Talk in character … Roll only when the GM calls for it"; four mode buttons, the four social checks of D&D, each showing its skill — **I persuade** / **I intimidate** / **I lie** / **I read them** (`_modeNow()`: ask | press | lie | read, from `_tTech` when one is picked, else `_tMode`). Nothing else to click until the GM calls a roll. I persuade (mode id `ask`) → "How did your words come at them?" (Heart or Reason, `data-t-ap`, each with how the person is KNOWN to take it: `TSLTalk.knownRelation` key → advantage, wall → disadvantage, plain → a normal roll, unknown) → "Plainly — or all in?" (the ask and the gambit of that approach; Reason has no gambit, so it opens on the Argument) → the picked maneuver: "If it works / If not", "What are you asking for?", "Who's watching?", "Did your words play on what you know?" (levers), help, the Roll button with the odds (`TSLTalk.chance` / `hitChance`: party knowledge only, never the truth); the result says what to do next. I intimidate (the user, 2026-10-31: «А ЗАЛЯКУВАННЯ?») → always Pressure, so nothing to pick: the form opens at once on Demand, showing how they're known to take it; "Plainly — or all in?" (Demand or Blackmail); "What do you demand?". I lie (the user, 2026-10-30: «а де брехня?» — a lie isn't an ask) → "How do you tell the lie?" (all three approaches, in the lie's own words) → its form: "How big is the lie?", no levers. Sizes keep their DC shifts in each one's words (`TSLTalk.sizeOf`). Never call the third approach "plain ground" in the UI — it's a normal roll.
- **The stake: Strings** (resolve; 5+ since 2026-11-04, the same both ways): a sized roll (ask or gambit — **not a lie** since 2026-11-13: what they believe is no hold on them) that's a **strong** win (5 over, or a 20) → a String on them (`_winHold` says why) — unless it went on the price (`onTerms`, below); a **botch** (5 under, or a 1) → a String on the speaker; and **any miss from the heart** (`tech.approach === "heart"`: Appeal, Seduce) → a String on the speaker ("You showed your heart"). A plain win or a plain miss moves none — but some wins cost one back (below). One each way, cap 1 (`STRING_CAP`) — at cap the card says "one at a time". A roll on which a String was spent (`stringSpent`, or the NPC's triad move) moves none. A read wins none (`readCost`: Confide's miss alone gives them one).
- **What a win costs** (applied in resolve) — **a roll never changes a bond** (neither its type nor its dots):
  - Pressure and Blackmail wins → no price, but they take a String on you ("They'll pay you back"); a Pressure win on a guilt type (Friend, Family, Lover, Protégé, Sworn, Confidant) with ● also leaves you Guilty;
  - a price that is a debt → a String on you;
  - Appeal/Argument plain hits → the nature's `price` prompt (`hasPrice`). **A strong Appeal/Argument keeps the price** (2026-11-13): the speaker picks in `promptPrice(…, {strong})` — pay it and keep the String, or "on my terms" (`payload.terms`, resolve's `onTerms`: no price, no String); with no String to win (capped, or one burned on the roll) it's on their terms at once. Three ways, three costs: Pressure pays when it wins, Reason pays the price, the heart pays when it loses.
  - A win **against the interests** of someone close (a guilt type with ●) leaves the speaker Guilty whatever the way — as Pressure on them always did.

A Read has no approach — only edge and cost count there. Dots grow only by invested Strings (the player) or the GM's hand; `shift` (warm/cool) moves the dots of a typed bond; `begin` gives a type where there's none or a Stranger. **On the token** (v3.15): within that reach the bond is a status on both of them, like any other — the type's icon tinted by its approach, "Rival ●● — Captain Roell", the aura inside; its own text says the rest (the aura it gives now, their guard, both Strings, ●● and whether ●●● is ready), and the SYSTEM lists it with its own statuses (a5e: the Effects Panel, for the token you control). Not a badge over the token (3.14; the user: «НЕ НАД ТОКЕНОМ») and not a panel of ours in a corner (3.14–3.17; the user: «ВІКНО СТАТУСУ В КУТУ ЕКРАНА, АБСОЛЮТНО НЕПОТРІБНЕ»).
- **Gambits:**
  - **No all-in is locked** (2026-11-13): what you hold over them, or what they long for, may have come from anyone — the GM decides if it bites; `preview().bare` = nothing of it on record, and the form says what the record would add (`bareOf`, `.tsl-pick-out--bare`).   - Blackmail: known Fear +2 (the lever) → hit: they're Frightened, and they take a String on you (whether they're your Enemy now is the GM's call); miss: their emotion.
  - Seduce: known Desire +2 → hit: they do it for you, no price — and they take a String on you ("You let them close": all in always binds); miss: a String on you and their emotion — Hopeless instead (`heartbreak`) only when it leaned on a bond of ●●+ with the Desire not played on.

A player raises dots only by investing (lowering is free); the GM sets them by hand; the "+" is the GM's. `TSLStringStore.add` makes a **Stranger** bond when none exists; investing a String in it (or the GM) gives it a type.
- **NPC moves** (`abilities.js`): each costs the NPC's String on that character. The triad's at the roll — `TSLAbilities.promptMove` on the GM in resolve, after the dice (Power any sized roll: −5 and re-grade; Emotion a win that stands: their `hurts` on the speaker; Order a win with no price paid already: their price). The nature's two from the NPC's Talk tab (`_movesSection`, "Pull it on: [PC]") → a chat card waiting for the answer (`by: "pc"` the PC's owner or GM; `"gm"` the GM only; Quits if the PC holds one on them); fx: inspire (`system.attributes.inspiration`), keep, flip, silence (+4 DC in preview until lifted), emotion, shift, pick friend/npc/lie (GM dialog), transfer. A move is a tell.
- **Roll** (speaker's client, `TSLTalk.roll`): `preview()` builds the visible math (the better skill + proficiency if trained in the other + the Leaning of that approach + bond ● + lever + help ± emotions vs DC `10 + prof + max(WIS, INT)` + dial + size). It never consults the nature. The system's check window opens by default (`systemRollDialog` true), set to the skill with the module's bonus as a situational mod. A KEY approach rolls a second d20 and keeps the better, a WALL keeps the worse — on the ask, the gambit and a lie told that way (a Read has no approach). **A state beats a habit** (2026-11-13; advantage since 2026-11-19): an emotion on them that opens this way (`TSLEmotions.opening`) gives advantage — `TSLTalk.relationFor(src, npc, approach)` → `{rel, down, open}`: against their wall `{rel: "plain", down}` (they cancel), on a normal way `{open}` (a second d20, the better); `chance().open` / `.down` for the odds, and the approach button says so; the card says why the wall didn't stand only when it was already known. Into a WALL the support doesn't count either (`wallLost`, after the dice; the user's pick, 2026-10-31: support doubles proficiency, so without this the best-trained face rolled 72% into a wall). `tools/harness.js` holds balance targets for levels 1–17; a rule change must stay inside them. Grades: strong (DC+5 / nat 20), success, failure, botch (DC−5 / nat 1). A miss may burn a String (+5). Then `TSLGMActions.request("talkResolve", payload)` with `technique`.
- **Resolve** (GM only): re-derives key/wall from the truth, records knowledge.
  - The stake (above): a strong win → a String on them; a botch → one on the speaker; one each way per roll; none when a String was spent on it.
  - Names the nature once key AND wall are known. ONE card, then a pulse `{technique, approach, job, grade, paid, rel, granted, witnesses}`.
- Whether they'll listen again, after a no, is narrative — the GM holds it.
- **Emotions** (`emotions.js`): an emotion is an AE with `statuses: ["tsl-emo-<id>"]` + `flags.emotion {id, by, fx}` (a HUD toggle without the flag counts too). **A character for a while, like a compulsion** (3.21; the user: «плюси на емоції мені теж не подобаються»; «Інакше вони будуть кидати аби що щоб позбавитися емоції. Це має бути важливим в рідкісним чинником»; «це може працювати десь як компульсія в втм»): a strength and a weakness, as advantage and disadvantage, on the sheet by itself — Angry: melee attacks + Intimidation ↑, Insight + Dex saves ↓; Frightened: initiative + Dex saves ↑, attacks + Intimidation ↓; Guilty: Persuasion + Insight ↑, Deception + initiative ↓; Insecure: Perception + Investigation ↑, Performance + Wis saves ↓; Hopeless: immune to frightened, Persuasion + death saves ↓. No number anywhere. In a talk the skill carries it: a5e's own skill check rolls it (the module reads kh/kl off the dice), the module's own d20 counts `pv.emo`; with the nature's key/wall/open it combines as D&D does (any advantage and any disadvantage → a normal roll; `rollMode`); the card says "{who} carries it in". The target's emotion that this approach plays on (`opens`, `TSLEmotions.opening`) is **advantage** — `open` on a normal way, `down` against their wall (a normal roll), nothing more on their key. **Rare** (the user's pick «Провал і ва-банк»): a bad miss (5 under, or a 1) of an ask or a lie → the nature's `hurts`; any miss of an all-in (a Seduce that leaned on the bond → Hopeless) or a Provoke → the same; a plain miss → none, but before witnesses → Insecure (only if no other came); a win against someone close (guilt type with ●) → Guilty; the NPC's triad move (feel-it); an NPC's move, refused; Blackmail → NPC Frightened; exposeLie → NPC Angry; the GM's mark from the talk (`mark`, no roll). **One at a time:** a new one pushes out the old; the same again stays (no scars since 3.22 — the user: «шрами приберемо»). **A drive, like a compulsion** (3.22): Angry → Revenge, Frightened → Escape, Guilty → Atonement, Insecure → Recognition, Hopeless → Oblivion — `clear` says when it's done. **It goes** only when its drive is done — the GM's word ("Acted it out" → `clear(…, "act")` → D&D Inspiration, `TSLAbilities.inspire`), a friend's comfort or "calmed" (no reward), or by itself after a **long rest** (`restCalm`, the rest hooks in bond-powers.js; the user's pick «До довгого відпочинку»). A player can't shed one: × / a lit tile → "I acted it out — ask the GM" (`requestLetGo`: a chat card, flag `letGo`, the GM's buttons — `bindLetGoCard`); they may undo only one they put on themselves (`canDrop`). **Shown at once:** the strip at the top of every Chronicle tab — the emotion; the tiles (first tab, and Change) show what each gives (↑), takes (↓) and drives to (») and, on an NPC, what it opens. Our menu holds the social statuses only (the user, 2026-11-09: «тільки статуси відносин, а не всі існуючі»); D&D's conditions stay with the system. All in is never locked; with nothing on record (no known Fear or String for Blackmail; no known Desire or ●● for Seduce) the form says so and what the record would add.
- **The deduction loop** (v1.7–v2's tells → guess → hints; lost in v3.0, back on 2026-11-14): every read that hits notices a SIGN (`pickTellKey`: one of forty the party hasn't got) — on the card, and KEPT (`socialFencing.signs`: English lines, the newest 12; each client reads them in its own language). The GM lets others show with no roll: the NPC's Talk tab, `_signsSection` — three to play, Others, "Let it show" → `TSLTalk.showSign` (a slim line). In a character's Talk (`_deduceHTML`, under what the party knows) the signs are listed and the party puts a GUESS forward ("Who are they, you think?" → `TSLTalk.guess` → the relay's `natureGuess` → `socialFencing.guess`): the guessed nature's `hint` shows, the approach buttons pencil in `guessRelation` wherever nothing is known (`relOf`, `.tsl-ap--guess`), and `chance()` follows it (`guessed`: "?" on the odds, "if your guess is right"). The roll follows the truth; resolve says when it fits the guess or doesn't. The GM sees the guess right or wrong and can confirm a right one. A named nature ends the guess. Show the block to the GM in a PC's Talk too — the author tests alone.
- **Knowledge:** `socialFencing.known {desire,fear,key,wall,price,audience}` + `revealed` — shared by the party, kept from then on. A named nature counts its card as known (key, wall, price, audience) — never `PERSONAL_FACTS` (desire, fear): those only by the GM's mark. Players never see the archetype (or the GM's dossier) until it is named; the GM sees the truth marked "hidden".

- Scene flag `board` — `{ pos: [{id, x, y}], notes: [{id, x, y, text}] }` (the GM's arrangement and the captions); the Web of Bonds keeps the same in the hidden world setting `worldBoard`. A player's own arrangement: localStorage.

## Hard-won platform lessons (still true)
- **`"socket": true` in module.json** is what makes Foundry relay `module.<id>` socket events. Without it every player → GM message is silently dropped. Changing it, or adding a new script file to the manifest, needs a **world restart**, not F5.
- **Refresh, don't re-render on everything.** The Chronicle listens to updateActor (only `flags.tsl-social-conflict`, name, img; `system` only on Talk), Active Effect hooks for its actor and target, updateScene, deleteActor — all through `_refresh()`: coalesced (60 ms) and held while a text field in the window has focus (a re-render wiped half-typed text). The Scene re-renders only for our effects. Keep new hooks on this path.
- **v14 draws an effect on a token only with `showIcon` ALWAYS (or a duration)** — v13 drew any effect with a status. Every status of ours sets it through `tslShowIcon()`; the GM's load sets it on older ones. v14 also moved `changes` to `system.changes` (with `type` for `mode`) — create effects with `changes` (migrated on creation) and never compare by reading them back. A token's state goes on the token as a status, like the system's own — the 3.14 badge over the token (HTML in `#hud`) was rejected. Never call `Token#isVisible` for a check — it clears the detection filter; read `token.visible`. **No status window of the module's on the map, ever** — a status goes where the SYSTEM lists statuses. a5e's Effects Panel (top-right, for the controlled token) lists only effects whose `system.effectType` is `condition` or `onUse`, or timed ones: ours say `condition` (`tslSystemStatus`; older ones are fixed on the GM's load). Its right-click removes a one-status effect through `Actor#toggleStatusEffect`, which throws for an id that isn't in CONFIG.statusEffects — so a status no system knows carries a second one (`tsl-social`) and is simply deleted; a bond in reach then comes straight back (the `deleteActiveEffect` hook). Don't register the bond types in CONFIG.statusEffects: a5e's own pickers list everything there.
- **a5e's long rest is `a5e.restCompleted`** (its RestManager, `{ data: { restType }, message }`; the actor is private — read it off the rest's chat message). `a5e.actorRest` never existed: the ●●● reset hung on it for a month and never fired.
- **Distances between tokens: edge to edge, from the document** (`x`, `y`, `width`, `height`) — centre to centre, a gargantuan dragon is 10 ft from its own skin; a token's placeable lags behind a move.
- **A status the GM can't see is a feature nobody sees.** The author tests with NPCs: a rule that hides something between two NPCs looks, at the table, like it doesn't work.
- **A bond aura is never left behind.** Anyone off the map loses it (the cleanup pass in `_refresh`). It works everywhere, in a fight or not (the user's call, 2026-11-06) — so its check and save bonuses count in social checks through the system window too.
- **The relay trusts no one's character but their own:** `TSLGMActions.handle(data, senderId)` checks the sender owns the actor `_actorOf` names (`srcId` / `holderId` / `actorId` / `pcId`; `stringSet` also checks the pcId is the holder or the target). A new GM action needs its actor in `_actorOf` — or a place in `OPEN` when it speaks for no one's character (`boardPatch`: captions on the shared board). With no GM online, `request` warns and returns false.
- **A player can't write an NPC's flags** — anything mirrored onto one (a bond's other side) goes through the GM, or it silently never happens (that was the bond desync before 2026-11-02).
- **Who is a PC:** always `SocialArchetypeManager.isPC(actor)` = type `"character"` OR player-owned — never `hasPlayerOwner` alone. The author tests alone as the only (GM) user, where nobody owns the characters; with `hasPlayerOwner` every character read as an NPC and Talk showed the GM cheat sheet instead of the ask (2026-10-14).
- The Chronicle normalises to the **world actor** (`game.actors.get(actor.id)`) — unlinked tokens hand over synthetic actors with token-local flags.
- Canvas picking uses a **DOM capture listener on `#board`** + `canvas.canvasCoordinatesFromClient` (PIXI 8 removed `canvas.app.view`).
- **a5e**: `system.bonuses.*` are RecordFields — a flat AE does nothing; numeric bonuses go through `flags.a5e.effects.bonuses.<kind>` (CUSTOM mode, the bonus record as JSON; list all four `attackTypes`). a5e roll-mode keys are `rollMode.{attack,abilityCheck,abilitySave,skillCheck}.all` (not `savingThrow`). AC: `system.attributes.ac.changes.bonuses.value`. dnd5e: `system.bonuses.*` / `system.attributes.ac.bonus`.
- Foundry gives `<button>` a stretchy base — every button needs an explicit width strategy.
- Look before claiming a UI works: `tools/preview.js --shoot` (portraits via env `TSL_PREVIEW_PORTRAITS`, never commit local paths). After an update mid-session, F5 — stale clients look broken.

## UI conventions
- CSS variables `--tsl-*` (dark: `--tsl-bg #150c0f`, text `#f0dfc8`, accent `#e8557a`); approach colours Pressure `#e8557a`, Heart `#9b6ee8`, Reason `#55b8e8`; gold `#e0b45e` (`--tsl-sway`, a legacy name) for sizes, prices and strong results; red `#d0574d` for walls and bad results. **One font family** for everything (2026-11-17 design pass — the user: «зовсім немає ніякого стилю і зручності»): `--tsl-title` = `--tsl-body` = Palatino Linotype → Book Antiqua → Palatino → Georgia, which carries Latin AND Cyrillic, so an English name and a Ukrainian word share a face; every button in our windows, dialogs and chat cards uses it too (Foundry's button face has no Cyrillic). Hierarchy by size, weight, case and colour — never by a second face. Never hard-code a font name in a rule; use the variables.
- **Layout rules** (same pass): small headings are all one style — uppercase 11–11.5px bold, letter-spaced, `--tsl-muted` (`.tsl-sec-title`, `.tsl-know-head`, `.tsl-row-label`, `.tsl-state-label`); the step questions of a roll (`.tsl-q`) are 13.5px bold, in plain sight; prose that explains how a screen works is `.tsl-note.tsl-help` (smaller, `--tsl-dim`, a thin rule on the left) — the data stays in full colour; a chip never breaks in two (`white-space: nowrap`, the effect text gives way first); the state strip above every tab but the first is compact (names only — the numbers in each chip's tooltip and in full on the Profile); the Codex's pages are one row of equal buttons (`--n`); the Strings between you sit on their own row under the target's name; a table never gets more columns than fit 560px.
- **Status badges** (`icons/badges/`, built by a script from Font Awesome Free solid glyphs — CC BY 4.0, every one credited in `icons/badges/LICENSE.md`; keep the README's credit): a token gives a status 20 px on a dark rounded square, so a badge is a **white, bold, simple glyph** filling the box, with a thin dark outline — no shape behind it, no colour (the user, 2026-11-19: «Надто кольорові. Надто невиразні, надто багато мілких деталей» — the coloured circles and squares of 3.20 shrank the glyph to mush). Judge one at its real 20 px on a token, next to a core status. Never set `tint` on ours. The Chronicle shows the same files; the colour of an emotion lives in the UI (a tile's border), not in the picture.
- **No emoji in UI** — Font Awesome icons (key = `fa-key`, wall = `fa-door-closed`, read = `fa-eye`). The user confirmed it on 2026-11-17, asked whether to bring back v1–v2's emoji: «ні, якраз не треба».
- Dialogs use `tslDialogOptions()` (dark theme). One card per roll; the result also shows where Roll was clicked.
- **Every word on screen goes through `_t("English", {placeholders})`** — a plain double-quoted literal (the harness scans for them). A new string needs its Ukrainian in `scripts/i18n-uk.js` (the harness fails on a missing key or a lost {placeholder}). Data texts (natures, techniques, emotions, bonds…) are translated in place by `TSLi18n.apply()` — add new data fields to it. Ukrainian style: the NPC is "вони" (no gendered verbs), the player "ти"; terms Струна, Зв'язок, Натура, Маска, Ключ/Стіна, При свідках, Просити/Ва-банк/Читати, СК, КБ. Chat cards are written in the language of the client that creates them.
- Cinzel and EB Garamond were never loaded, and Modesto Condensed / Signika carry no Cyrillic: until 3.19 a line mixed three faces (Latin names condensed caps, Ukrainian in Palatino, chat cards in Times). Keep the one Cyrillic-capable stack; Ukrainian labels are longer — check `--lang=uk` screenshots of every surface after a UI change.
