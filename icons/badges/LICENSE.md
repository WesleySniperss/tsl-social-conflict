# Status badges

The module's status icons: a white glyph with a thin dark outline, so it reads at the 20 px a token gives
a status. The glyphs are from [Font Awesome Free](https://fontawesome.com/) 6.5.2 by Fonticons, Inc.,
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the badges built from them are
part of TSL: Social Conflict (CC BY-SA 4.0).
