# TSL: Social Conflict

A social-intrigue toolkit for **Foundry VTT v13+** (v14 verified): a **Chronicle of Bonds** (relationships & profiling), a **Social Fencing** minigame (d20 maneuvers vs a hidden difficulty), and a live **Social Scene** relationship map. Inspired by the emotional mechanics of **Thirsty Sword Lesbians** and the social maneuvering of Vampire: the Masquerade. Built for **dnd5e** and **a5e** (Level Up), with a generic fallback.

## Three windows

- **Chronicle** — right-click a token → the address-book button. A per-character dossier, and where you actually fence (the **Fencing** tab). The **Codex** tab is the full, always-current in-app manual (paged, collapsible, with a worked example scene).
- **Social Scene** — the VTools toolbar **Social** button (everyone can open it). A live relationship map: portraits as nodes, bonds as the lines between them (colour = school, thickness = strength), with live roll/effect pulses (a beam, a flash, the falling Resolve, the swayed / broke-off drama). Drag the portraits to arrange it; **zoom the map** with the buttons or scroll-wheel (the window stays put, the map scrolls inside). It comes in **two views**: *This scene* (only the current scene — what players see) and *All bonds* (the whole web of relationships, GM overview) — switch with the header button; both can be open at once. The GM starts a shared conflict from the **⚔ Conflict** button here.
- **Conflict window** — the shared roll board when the GM launches a scene; every client sees it and acts from their own copy.

## Chronicle of Bonds

- **Profile** — the character's **archetype** (9 natures across the Power / Emotion / Reason triads — hidden from players by default), their Extended-Triad **dots** (a 4-point pool, PC-only), and the **profiling dossier**: **Desire / Fear / Weakness** are leverage doors you exploit in a conflict; **Mask / The Line** help you read them. Plus a GM-only **Agenda** (their goal in the scene) and free **Notes**. Hover any label for its hint.
- **Bonds** — one shared relationship per pair, **mirrored on both actors**: a **type** (ally, friend, family, crush, lover, mentor, protégé, rival, enemy, sworn, liege, confidant) and a **strength ●–●●●**. A bond bends the numbers (a school edge and skill edges on maneuvers, the guard DC, guilt when you turn Power on someone close), projects a **combat aura** to allies in reach, unlocks a distinctive **ability at ●●** and a once-per-rest **signature** at ●●●. Add a bond from the scene list or by clicking a token on the map.
- Players open only chronicles of actors they own; what they know about anyone else lives in their own Bonds.

## Social Fencing

- **Fifteen maneuvers in four schools.** **General** holds the basics anyone reaches for — read · mock · goad · **persuade** · **threaten** · **lie** — with no archetype traps; the three archetype schools (**Power · Emotion · Reason**) are the deeper game. Each maneuver rolls a main social skill; if you're **trained** in its support skill, your proficiency bonus rides on top. A **natural 1** always misses.
- **Resolve vs Patience — a real duel.** Everyone in an exchange has two tracks. **Resolve** (CHA modifier, floor 1 — force of personality) is your will: landed maneuvers chip it, and at 0 you're **swayed** — you concede (the big loss). **Patience** (WIS + CHA modifier, floor 2) is your composure: **your own misses spend it** (risky moves spend 2), and so does **every parry you make** — when a hit lands the defender may *take it* (lose Resolve), *parry* (1 Patience blocks 1 Resolve) or *riposte* (block it all and knock 1 Patience off the attacker). At 0 Patience you **break off** — you lose the exchange, but concede nothing. So pressing costs you and defending costs you: nobody wins by walling up. The winner takes a String; the loser's bond toward them deepens (swayed) or cools (broke off); a winning NPC's agenda advances. A school a target is **vulnerable** to can't be parried — a full concession usually needs their weak spot; one they're **immune** to slides off and costs you like a miss. Tracks arm on the first maneuver — no setup — and an exchange ends with its scene (or a GM reset). Resolve stays low: the weight is the maneuver's **school** (General 1 · archetype 2 · Humiliate 3).
- **Hidden difficulty & the deduction loop.** The social DC (10 + their WIS save + INT save, or passive Insight) is GM-only. Archetypes are hidden too: a successful **Read Them** whispers a tell, you write your best guess in the target's Bond (*Read as…*), and the weak/strong marks (◎/✕/▲) then follow **your theory** — provisional, so a wrong guess shows wrong marks and the *outcome* corrects you. No guess yet → no marks. The dice always resolve against the truth.
- **Nine natures, grounded in real psychology.** The three triads follow Karen Horney's three ways of coping with people — *against* them (Power: Tyrant · Schemer · Duelist), *toward* them (Emotion: Martyr · Idol · Caretaker), *away* from them (Reason: Zealot · Hermit · Broker). Each nature names the character type it's drawn from (the authoritarian narcissist, Machiavellianism, Reich's phallic-narcissist, the masochistic 'help-rejecting complainer', the histrionic, Bowlby's compulsive caregiver, the obsessive, the schizoid, Fromm's marketing orientation) and spells out what it's **strong** against and **weak** to. Every nature is vulnerable to some maneuvers (advantage, deeper damage, unparryable) and immune to others (press one and they turn **Defiant**). **Openings (⊕):** a condition on the target makes a matching maneuver stronger.
- **Sixteen maneuvers, each with a real tactic behind it** — cold reading, ingratiation, triangulation, guilt induction, sowing doubt, strategic use of evidence, reciprocity, appeal to authority (the new **Invoke Authority**: doubt first, then hand them a higher rule)… The Codex shows every move's strong and weak side, who it cuts and who it bounces off, plus a content note on the ones that model real abuse tactics.
- **States vs Wounds — and they know who you are to each other.** Fleeting fencing **States** (Rattled, Provoked, Enthralled, Beholden, Desperate — all one-shots — and Defiant) carry real combat riders and linger even after the talk. A state from someone who matters in the right way **runs deep** (two uses: a crush's charm, a mentor's doubt, a rival's goading); from someone they're set against it **won't take** (you can't charm an enemy). Lasting emotional **Wounds** (Wrath, Grudge, Obsession, Fear, Despair) escalate through three tiers and push the one who carries them — **give in** to the urge to regain Willpower. Wounds weighing **4+** (sum of tiers) = **Overwhelmed**: no parrying, no holding the line. A long rest eases a Wound one tier; one left at ●●● calcifies — into a permanent **Scar** if it's about you, into a **bond** if it's about someone (Obsession → a Crush, Grudge → an Enemy). On A5E, Wounds carried out of a conflict-window ending add **Strife**.
- **Strings** — a trump card: spend one for **+5** on any roll against that person, or **+5 to your AC / a save** against theirs. At most **3** on any one person. Earned by opening your heart in play, by maneuvers that hand you a lever (reads, Lie, Play Weak, Bargain…), or by winning an exchange.
- **Your triad dots, shown not hidden.** Besides +1 per dot on their school's maneuvers, the dots add to an everyday skill on your sheet (Power → Intimidation, Emotion → Insight, Reason → Deception); the Profile lists it and every roll bar says "incl. +N leaning".
- **Bonds move with the outcome, type-aware.** Swayed draws the loser closer, breaking off cools it — but for an **Enemy** or **Rival** it runs the other way (being swayed eases the hostility, breaking off hardens it).
- **Three plain tones of pressure, each its own tool.** **Persuade** is sincere — only 1 Resolve, but it **can't be parried** (the one way to sway someone who walls up everything); **Intimidate** hits hard (−2) but a miss costs you 2 Patience; **Lie** hands you a String, but a bad miss gets you **caught** — they take a String on you.
- **NPCs defend on their own.** Each NPC meets a landed blow by its **Defence stance** — Open, Measured, Guarded or Proud (by default from its nature: Power → Proud, Emotion → Measured, Reason → Guarded) — so the GM gets no window; set *Ask me* on the ones you want to steer. Player characters always decide.
- **Basic or full emotional layer** (world setting). *Full*: Wounds plus Willpower, Ultimates, Give in, Boons, Scars and the bonds' abilities/signatures. *Basic*: the Wounds alone.
- **One window to defend.** When a blow lands, the defender answers in a single window: take or parry it (and riposte), and — if it carries a state — accept it or **Hold the Line** (refuse the state by carrying a Wound; the blow itself still has to be met). The GM is asked to confirm a roll only on a close call (within 2 of the DC) or a natural 1.
- Optional layers: the **TSL 2d6 feelings moves**, Hold the Line, GM adjudication, and a global **Social difficulty** dial in Settings.

## Usage

1. **Prep** — right-click a token → the Chronicle → fill in Profile & Bonds. Hover any label for a hint; read the **Codex** tab for the full walkthrough.
2. **Fence** — open a character's Chronicle → **Fencing** tab → pick a target, pick a maneuver, roll. (Or the GM opens the **Social** scene and hits **⚔ Conflict** to launch a shared board.)
3. Tracks start automatically. Break their Resolve to **sway** them, or make them parry until their Patience is gone and they **break off** — but every miss spends *your* Patience, and if yours runs out first, you break off. Read targets to reveal tells; spend Strings for +5.
4. The exchange ends on **swayed**, **broke off**, a **Yield**, or a **Finally Kiss**. Any fencing statuses linger into a fight that follows.

Requires the **vtools** module.
